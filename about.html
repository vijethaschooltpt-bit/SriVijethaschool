About Sri Vijetha School

Sri Vijetha School describes its learning experience around strong foundations, joyful participation, creativity, movement, teamwork and character development.

What is the Sri Vijetha School learning approach?

The approach presented on this website combines classroom learning with opportunities to explore, create, play and work with others. The aim is to help children build knowledge and confidence together.

Who is this information for?

This page is for parents and guardians who want a clear overview of the school’s stated approach before making an admissions enquiry.
