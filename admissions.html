Admissions & Enquiries

Parents and guardians can contact Sri Vijetha School to ask about admissions, classes, availability, school routines and other information relevant to their child.

How can I enquire about admission?

Call +91 7780574086 or return to the home page and use the enquiry form.

What should I ask the school?

  • Which classes are currently accepting enquiries?
  • What documents or information are required?
  • What are the school timings and admission steps?
  • What learning and activity opportunities are available?

Important admission information

Admission availability, fees, dates, required documents and eligibility can change. Please confirm current details directly with the school before making a decision.
